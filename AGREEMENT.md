## 1. This is test software

BoothMaster Beta is pre-release software, given to you only so that you can test it and tell us what you find. It is not finished. It will have bugs, some features may not work, and it can change or stop working without notice. It is not for everyday or professional use.

## 2. It has not been fully tested on DJ equipment

BoothMaster has not been comprehensively tested on professional DJ equipment (players, mixers and all-in-one systems from Pioneer DJ, AlphaTheta or anyone else), with every Rekordbox version, or with every kind of library. A library or USB stick that BoothMaster has worked on may not behave on a given player the way you expect.

## 3. Two ways to use BoothMaster with your library

You choose how BoothMaster works with your library. Moved to BoothMaster (recommended): BoothMaster copies your Rekordbox library into a library of its own, and from then on reads and writes only that one. Your Rekordbox library is read once, from a copy, and BoothMaster never writes to it again. From the move on, the two libraries are separate: Rekordbox does not see the changes you make in BoothMaster, BoothMaster does not see the changes you make in Rekordbox, and BoothMaster cannot copy its changes back into Rekordbox. If you start without a Rekordbox library, BoothMaster makes a library of its own. Alongside Rekordbox: BoothMaster reads and writes Rekordbox's own library (its database, playlists, cue points, beat grids, tags and analysis files). Every change shows in both apps, but a bug or a mistake can leave your Rekordbox library incorrect, incomplete or damaged. We recommend moving to BoothMaster, because then BoothMaster never writes to your Rekordbox library. You can move at any time in Settings > Your library.

## 4. Your music files and USB sticks

Either way, BoothMaster works on the music files on your disk and on USB sticks. A bug or a mistake can leave a library incorrect, incomplete or damaged, can move, change or delete files, and can leave a USB stick that does not play as you expect. Your music files are shared: after a move, Rekordbox's library still points at the same files. If you then use BoothMaster to move or delete files, for example to merge duplicates or to consolidate your library, Rekordbox is not told, and will show those tracks as missing.

## 5. Always back up first

Before you use BoothMaster, every time, back up the library it works in and keep a copy of your music files. Alongside Rekordbox, that is your Rekordbox library (in Rekordbox: File > Library > Backup Library). Moved to BoothMaster, it is BoothMaster's library folder (Settings > Your library shows where it is): copy it somewhere else, such as another disk. BoothMaster makes its own copy of the database before it writes, but that is not a substitute for yours.

## 6. At events, carry a backup USB made by Rekordbox

If you play an event with anything BoothMaster has prepared or changed, you must also carry a backup USB that was exported directly from Rekordbox, not from BoothMaster, and check that it plays before the event. If you have moved to BoothMaster, remember that this backup will not have the changes you made in BoothMaster since the move.

## 10. What the beta sends

To check that your licence is still valid, and to get messages and updates about the beta, the beta contacts the BoothMaster server (run by the developer and hosted by Fly.io) when it starts, every few hours, and when you ask it to check. Before you have a licence it sends only details of the app: its build and version, and your operating system. Once you have one, it also sends your licence, which holds your name, e-mail address, licence ID and end date, an ID for this computer and this installation, and the version of this agreement you signed. The server keeps a record of your licence and when it last heard from it, and it sees your internet (IP) address. If you accept a record label's invitation to receive promos, the beta also sends what happens to that label's promo tracks, and nothing else: when they are downloaded, analysed, put on or taken off a USB stick (with the stick's name and the file's place on it), and played, and where. The label that sent them may see this for its own tracks. The beta does not send the rest of your library, your other tracks or your other play history, except about record labels. When BoothMaster finds a record label's website for you, or you type one in, it sends that label's name and the website's address, so that the developer can check it and share the right ones with other testers. To find a website, the beta or the BoothMaster server asks MusicBrainz, a public music database, with the label's name only. For label pages, the beta tells the BoothMaster server which record labels are in your library; the server finds each label's public website, reads it and shares that label's page with every tester who has the label. Information about the record labels in your library, and about your tracks on them, is kept centrally on the BoothMaster server, and may be used within BoothMaster and shared with the label concerned. When a newer beta is out, the app offers to download it from the beta's page on GitHub and install it. This information is used to run the beta, its licences, its promos and its label pages, and is shared only as this section says. Your access request holds your name, e-mail address, an ID for this computer and installation, and the time you signed this agreement; you send it to us yourself.
