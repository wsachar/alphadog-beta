Version 3. This is the text you sign in AlphaDog Beta.

## 1. This is test software

AlphaDog Beta is pre-release software, given to you only so that you can test it and tell us what you find. It is not finished. It will have bugs, some features may not work, and it can change or stop working without notice. It is not for everyday or professional use.

## 2. It has not been fully tested on DJ equipment

AlphaDog has not been comprehensively tested on professional DJ equipment (players, mixers and all-in-one systems from Pioneer DJ, AlphaTheta or anyone else), with every Rekordbox version, or with every kind of library. A library or USB stick that AlphaDog has worked on may not behave on a given player the way you expect.

## 3. It changes your library

AlphaDog reads and writes your Rekordbox library: the database, playlists, cue points, beat grids, tags, the track files on disk, and USB sticks. A bug or a mistake can leave a library incorrect, incomplete or damaged, and can move, change or delete files.

## 4. Always back up your library first

Before you use AlphaDog, every time, back up your Rekordbox library (in Rekordbox: File > Library > Backup Library) and keep a copy of your music files. AlphaDog makes its own copy of the database before it writes, but that is not a substitute for yours.

## 5. At events, carry a backup USB made by Rekordbox

If you play an event with anything AlphaDog has prepared or changed, you must also carry a backup USB that was exported directly from Rekordbox, not from AlphaDog, and check that it plays before the event.

## 9. What the beta sends

To check that your licence is still valid, and to get messages and updates about the beta, the beta contacts the AlphaDog server (run by the developer and hosted by Fly.io) when it starts, every few hours, and when you ask it to check. Before you have a licence it sends only details of the app: its build and version, and your operating system. Once you have one, it also sends your licence, which holds your name, e-mail address, licence ID and end date, an ID for this computer and this installation, and the version of this agreement you signed. The server keeps a record of your licence and when it last heard from it, and it sees your internet (IP) address. If you accept a record label's invitation to receive promos, the beta also sends what happens to that label's promo tracks, and nothing else: when they are downloaded, analysed, put on or taken off a USB stick (with the stick's name and the file's place on it), and played, and where. The label that sent them may see this for its own tracks. The beta never sends anything about the rest of your library, your other tracks or your other play history. When a newer beta is out, the app offers to download it from the beta's page on GitHub and install it. This information is used only to run the beta, its licences and its promos. Your access request holds your name, e-mail address, an ID for this computer and installation, and the time you signed this agreement; you send it to us yourself.
