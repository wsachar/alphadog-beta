# AlphaDog Beta Test Agreement

Version 4. This is the text you sign in AlphaDog Beta.

Please read this before you use AlphaDog. To use this beta you need to accept every point below and sign with your full name.

## 1. This is test software

AlphaDog Beta is pre-release software, given to you only so that you can test it and tell us what you find. It is not finished. It will have bugs, some features may not work, and it can change or stop working without notice. It is not for everyday or professional use.

## 2. It has not been fully tested on DJ equipment

AlphaDog has not been comprehensively tested on professional DJ equipment (players, mixers and all-in-one systems from Pioneer DJ, AlphaTheta or anyone else), with every Rekordbox version, or with every kind of library. A library or USB stick that AlphaDog has worked on may not behave on a given player the way you expect.

## 3. Two ways to use AlphaDog with your library

You choose how AlphaDog works with your library. Moved to AlphaDog (recommended): AlphaDog copies your Rekordbox library into a library of its own, and from then on reads and writes only that one. Your Rekordbox library is read once, from a copy, and AlphaDog never writes to it again. From the move on, the two libraries are separate: Rekordbox does not see the changes you make in AlphaDog, AlphaDog does not see the changes you make in Rekordbox, and AlphaDog cannot copy its changes back into Rekordbox. If you start without a Rekordbox library, AlphaDog makes a library of its own. Alongside Rekordbox: AlphaDog reads and writes Rekordbox's own library (its database, playlists, cue points, beat grids, tags and analysis files). Every change shows in both apps, but a bug or a mistake can leave your Rekordbox library incorrect, incomplete or damaged. We recommend moving to AlphaDog, because then AlphaDog never writes to your Rekordbox library. You can move at any time in Settings > Your library.

## 4. Your music files and USB sticks

Either way, AlphaDog works on the music files on your disk and on USB sticks. A bug or a mistake can leave a library incorrect, incomplete or damaged, can move, change or delete files, and can leave a USB stick that does not play as you expect. Your music files are shared: after a move, Rekordbox's library still points at the same files. If you then use AlphaDog to move or delete files, for example to merge duplicates or to consolidate your library, Rekordbox is not told, and will show those tracks as missing.

## 5. Always back up first

Before you use AlphaDog, every time, back up the library it works in and keep a copy of your music files. Alongside Rekordbox, that is your Rekordbox library (in Rekordbox: File > Library > Backup Library). Moved to AlphaDog, it is AlphaDog's library folder (Settings > Your library shows where it is): copy it somewhere else, such as another disk. AlphaDog makes its own copy of the database before it writes, but that is not a substitute for yours.

## 6. At events, carry a backup USB made by Rekordbox

If you play an event with anything AlphaDog has prepared or changed, you must also carry a backup USB that was exported directly from Rekordbox, not from AlphaDog, and check that it plays before the event. If you have moved to AlphaDog, remember that this backup will not have the changes you made in AlphaDog since the move.

## 10. What the beta sends

To check that your licence is still valid, and to get messages and updates about the beta, the beta contacts the AlphaDog server (run by the developer and hosted by Fly.io) when it starts, every few hours, and when you ask it to check. Before you have a licence it sends only details of the app: its build and version, and your operating system. Once you have one, it also sends your licence, which holds your name, e-mail address, licence ID and end date, an ID for this computer and this installation, and the version of this agreement you signed. The server keeps a record of your licence and when it last heard from it, and it sees your internet (IP) address. If you accept a record label's invitation to receive promos, the beta also sends what happens to that label's promo tracks, and nothing else: when they are downloaded, analysed, put on or taken off a USB stick (with the stick's name and the file's place on it), and played, and where. The label that sent them may see this for its own tracks. The beta never sends anything about the rest of your library, your other tracks or your other play history. When a newer beta is out, the app offers to download it from the beta's page on GitHub and install it. This information is used only to run the beta, its licences and its promos. Your access request holds your name, e-mail address, an ID for this computer and installation, and the time you signed this agreement; you send it to us yourself.
